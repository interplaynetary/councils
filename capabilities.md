
# 🌟 Distributed Council System: Capabilities Guide

Welcome to the **Distributed Council System**. This platform is designed to enable groups to self-organize, make decisions, and execute actions securely and democratically.

Unlike traditional governance tools that just record votes, this system is **alive**—it holds the power to execute the decisions you make.

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

### 1. **Secure Association (No Passwords)**
You don't "log in" with a username and password. instead, you conduct a handshake to establish a **Secure Session**.
-   **What you can do**: Join a council instantly.
-   **How it works**: You receive a digital key (Capability) that proves who you are. If you misbehave, this key can be revoked instantly, locking you out without needing to change passwords.

### 2. **Real Execution (The "Muscle")**
Most voting apps are just polls. When a vote passes, someone still has to go do the work manually. **Not here.**
-   **What you can do**: Create proposals that *contain* the actual action.
-   **Example**: "Propose to send a payment" or "Propose to post a message to the Farmers Council."
-   **The Magic**: When the vote passes (hits quorum), the system **automatically executes** the action. No admins required.

### 3. **Liquid Democracy (Fluid Power)**
You have a voice, but you don't always have the time.
-   **What you can do**: Delegate your voting power to someone you trust (e.g., Alice).
-   **How it works**: If you delegate to Alice, and Alice delegates to Bob, then Bob votes with the power of all three of you.
-   **Freedom**: You can take your power back or switch delegates at any time.

### 4. **Cross-Network Cooperation**
Your council isn't an island. It can interact with other councils or systems.
-   **What you can do**: Give your council a "capability" (like a phone line) to talk to another council.
-   **Example**: The **Workers Council** can vote to send a formal message to the **Farmers Council**.
-   **Security**: These connections are secure. You can revoke the connection if the relationship sours.

---

## 🛡️ Safety Features

-   **Zero-Trust Security**: Everything is wrapped in secure layers. Even if a proposer tries to sneak in a raw access key, the system automatically wraps it in a safety layer.
-   **Instant Revocation**:
    -   **Member Ban**: Admins can cut off a bad actor's session instantly.
    -   **Grant Revocation**: Did you grant a permission to a proposal that you now regret? You can revoke that specific grant without breaking the whole system.

---

## 🔮 The Philosophy
This system is built on **Object Capabilities**. Access is not about *who you are*, but *what you hold*. This makes the system robust, composable, and extremely secure by default.
